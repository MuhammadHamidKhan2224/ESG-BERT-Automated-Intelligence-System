NEXUS GLOBAL ENERGY CORP - ANNUAL SUSTAINABILITY & ESG DISCLOSURE 2024

Letter from the Board & CEO:
Nexus Global Energy is committed to building a resilient, low-carbon future while generating long-term shareholder value. Over the course of 2024, our global business accelerated capital deployment into renewable power generation and grid modernization.

Environmental Stewardship & Climate Action:
We achieved a 28% reduction in combined Scope 1 and Scope 2 greenhouse gas emissions across all refining and power generation assets.
Over the past fiscal year, 42% of our total electricity generation portfolio originated from zero-emission wind, solar, and battery storage systems.
Our engineers completed methane leak detection and repair (LDAR) surveys across 100% of our active pipeline infrastructure, abating 18,000 metric tons of CO2 equivalent.
Nexus invested $140 million in circular water recycling technologies, which enabled our manufacturing facilities to reduce freshwater intake by 35%.
We strive to reach net-zero greenhouse gas emissions across our operations by 2050, in alignment with the Paris Climate Agreement goals.
We completed comprehensive biodiversity risk impact assessments for all new offshore and terrestrial infrastructure projects.

Social Responsibility & Human Capital:
Our workforce is our greatest asset, and we remain dedicated to maintaining world-class workplace health and safety standards.
In 2024, our Total Recordable Incident Rate (TRIR) decreased by 22% year-over-year, reaching an industry-leading 0.31 incidents per 200,000 hours worked.
We increased the representation of women in technical and engineering leadership roles to 38%, up from 29% in 2021.
Nexus invested $18 million in continuous workforce retraining, safety certifications, and technical apprenticeship programs.
All primary equipment suppliers and contractors were required to adhere to our human rights charter prohibiting forced labor, child labor, and wage abuses.
The Nexus Community Foundation distributed $9.4 million in direct grants to fund local clean drinking water infrastructure and educational STEM scholarships.

Corporate Governance, Board Oversight & Ethics:
The Board of Directors maintains an independent lead director and consists of 75% independent non-executive directors.
Executive annual incentive compensation is directly tied to measurable ESG performance targets, including greenhouse gas emission cuts and safety metrics.
The Audit Committee oversees financial controls, enterprise risk management, and our comprehensive cybersecurity defense operations.
100% of full-time employees and executives completed our mandatory annual anti-corruption, anti-bribery, and business ethics training.
Our confidential third-party whistleblower helpline operates 24/7, with all flagged matters reported directly to the Audit Committee.
We engage regularly with institutional shareholders to solicit feedback on executive remuneration, board diversity, and ESG disclosures.

Financial Performance & Market Outlook:
During the fiscal year, revenue reached $14.2 billion, driven by sustained demand and favorable natural gas pricing.
Operating income expanded by 8.4%, while capital expenditures totaled $2.1 billion.
We returned $1.2 billion to shareholders through quarterly dividend distributions and share buyback programs.
